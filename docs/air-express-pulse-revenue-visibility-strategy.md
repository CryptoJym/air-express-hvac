## TL;DR

- The highest-impact Air Express growth lane is not a standalone generic news
  hub first. It is a weekly Utah County Home Comfort Pulse that feeds a seasonal
  summer blog series, service-area proof pages, and a case-study pipeline.
- The strategy must start with entity and trust cleanup because public surfaces
  appear to mix Air Express, One Hour, older domains, review counts, and
  directory claims. Those conflicts can weaken SEO, GEO, and AI-answer trust.
- Prepared content should support AC repair, AC replacement, tune-ups,
  maintenance plans, indoor air quality, emergency calls, and city pages.
  Stronger proof pages wait for Max-approved jobs, photos, testimonials,
  license/review proof, and redacted ServiceTitan/source evidence.
- Measurement remains directional until GSC/GA4/GBP read access, live GA4
  delivery, and ServiceTitan export joins are restored. Weekly reporting should
  separate visibility movement from lead and revenue proof.

## Recommended Strategy

Primary asset: weekly Utah County Home Comfort Pulse.

Format:

- 3 verified local signals.
- 1 homeowner action.
- 1 service CTA.
- 1 trust or proof note.
- 1 status line that says whether the item is prepared, approved, published,
  live, or verified.

Monthly support asset: Rebates, IAQ, And Equipment Watch.

This should cover rebate/tax-credit changes, utility programs, air-quality
conditions, refrigerant transition, equipment replacement questions, and local
housing/remodel signals. Do not publish rebate, savings, health, utility, or
tax-credit claims without checking the current official source on the publish
date.

Content should flow from the pulse into:

- Seasonal blog posts that answer urgent homeowner questions.
- High-intent service pages and city pages.
- Proof-backed authority pages once Max supplies real evidence.
- Weekly AI/GEO prompt tracking.
- Pages-only reporting that shows what changed, what was published, and what
  moved in GSC, GA4, GBP, AI visibility, and ServiceTitan once those joins exist.

## Weekly Pulse Source Stack

Use these sources for the weekly pulse. Record source date/time for any
temporary condition.

| Signal | Official or durable source | Claim gate |
|---|---|---|
| Heat readiness and no-cool surge timing | NWS Salt Lake City, NOAA CPC outlooks | Say "heat wave" only when the weather source supports it. |
| Wildfire smoke and PM2.5 | AirNow Fire and Smoke Map, Utah DEQ air forecast, EPA wildfire IAQ guidance | No health-outcome promises. Distinguish smoke/PM2.5 from ozone. |
| Ozone and poor-air-action days | Utah DEQ and AirNow | Keep advice homeowner-safe and source current conditions. |
| Rebates and tax credits | IRS, Rocky Mountain Power Wattsmart, Enbridge Utah, Utah Office of Energy Development | The IRS Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit page says qualifying improvements are claimable through 2025; do not imply 2026 federal credit availability without current verification. |
| Refrigerant transition | EPA HFC phasedown and AHRI transition resources | Do not say older systems are illegal or instantly obsolete. |
| Maintenance timing | ENERGY STAR HVAC maintenance checklist, DOE Energy Saver | Keep recommendations general unless Air Express approves its exact tune-up scope. |
| Utility and outage context | Rocky Mountain Power, Lehi City where applicable | Do not predict grid events or outages without a utility source. |
| Growth, remodels, and new construction | Census Building Permits Survey, city permit sources, Kem C. Gardner Policy Institute | Treat permits as demand signals, not proof of Air Express revenue. |
| Contractor trust and licensing | Utah DOPL license lookup and Utah consumer-protection sources | Do not make competitor accusations. Verify Air Express license/review facts before public reuse. |

## Proof Gates

Do not publish or reuse these as public claims until approved evidence is in
the repo-local evidence packet:

- BBB accreditation or BBB rating.
- Exact Google review count or rating.
- "Best," "most trusted," "top rated," or similar superlative claims.
- Response-time, same-day, emergency, or 24/7 operating claims beyond approved
  Air Express language.
- Revenue, lead, conversion, or customer outcome claims.
- Tax-credit/rebate availability for 2026.
- Health outcomes from filters, IAQ, purifiers, or wildfire-smoke guidance.
- Customer photos, named case studies, testimonials, or job outcomes.
- ServiceTitan lead/revenue impact.

Needed proof:

- Max-approved job stories and permission status.
- Redacted ServiceTitan/source notes by date, source, campaign, status,
  booking, and revenue if available.
- Approved photos or no-photo alternatives.
- Current license/certification proof.
- Current review/rating source proof.
- Approved relationship language for One Hour, if applicable.
- Approved dispatch, tune-up, and emergency-service wording.
